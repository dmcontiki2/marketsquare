Tuppence allowances

  • Card: First-lister bonus · Tuppence grant.
  • Three kinds: first-lister bonus (once per person), tester grant, goodwill.
  • 1 to 500T per grant.
  • Never expires and never counts as sales income.
  • Every grant is written to an audit log with your name.
  • Used so far: one 25T first-lister bonus and one 500T tester grant.

Approvals

  • Confirm or reject a seller's ID document.
  • Approve or reject a licence / qualification claim.
  • Uphold a buyer's complaint (lowers the seller's Trust Score).
  • The server has all three, but only a technical call reaches them — nothing on the +1 page.

Services and plans

  • No way to give someone a paid plan or more listing slots for free.
  • No way to give free AI services to one person (a Tuppence grant does the same job today).
  • "Full access" accounts are fixed in code for your four family test accounts plus one more.

How to give Tuppence — step by step

  1. Open your dashboard and sign in with your admin PIN.
  2. Click the Launch Switch tab (the +1 page).
  3. Scroll to the card First-lister bonus · Tuppence grant. It lists real people who listed for the first time in the last 30 days, marked no bonus yet or 25T paid.
  4. Type part of the person's name or email (any email used in the app) and choose her from the list.
  5. Check the box that appears: her name, plan, balance now, live listings, ID status and grants already given.
  6. Enter the amount, choose the kind, click Credit Tuppence. The confirm shows her balance now and after.
