1  The correction this map encodes

A human in a throughput path is a defect, not a control

The obvious reading of the Pizza Hut and Klarna failures is “put a human back where the AI decides.” On a high-volume surface that is wrong twice over. It caps the business at one person’s rate — and the control decays: a reviewer at 500 photos a day is rubber-stamping by day three. That leaves a control which only looks like a control, which is worse than none, because it is trusted. AI has no such failure mode: item 500 is judged exactly like item 1.

Klarna does not contradict this. Their error was capability mismatch — AI put on emotionally-charged, multi-step resolution it could not do, for cost reasons, while claiming quality. MarketSquare’s AI surfaces are classification, extraction, comparison and drafting at volume: the things AI genuinely does better.

Pizza Hut does not either. Dragon Tail’s failure was not a missing shift manager — it was that nobody modelled how two independently optimising systems would interact once the AI changed the information environment. That is systems modelling, and it would have failed with a human in the chair.

2  What catches a wrong answer

Ranked by how well each preserves throughput. A human appears last, and only for exceptions whose class is legal, financial, partner or infrastructural.

1

Deterministic guard

Bounds, schema, allow-list, idempotency. Cheapest and most reliable. Costs no throughput.

2

Second AI lane, different vendor -- DISAGREEMENT is the exception signal

The +1 provider slot already exists for failover and sits idle in normal operation. On RED surfaces it can double as an adversarial checker: run both lanes, and treat DISAGREEMENT -- not low confidence -- as the exception. Preserves throughput, uses AI's strength to police AI, and turns dormant failover capacity into a live control.

3

Economic absorber

Refund, reserve, or insurance so a wrong answer costs money rather than trust. Money is recoverable; trust is not.

4

AI-run appeal route

The appeal is itself handled by AI. Only the residue -- the cases the appeal cannot close -- reaches a human interface role.

5

Human interface role

LAST, and only for exceptions whose CLASS is legal, financial, partner or infrastructural. Never for throughput.

3  The four human interface roles

Humans own boundaries where MarketSquare meets a party that can compel it — a party requiring a named, accountable person. Each role owns exceptions, never throughput.

R1

Legal & Regulatory Interface

Owns: What the AI is ALLOWED to say and do. POPIA / consumer law / financial-advice boundary, EULA and disclosure wording, dispute-of-record, counsel and regulator liaison, tax and VAT classification of affiliate income.

Why human: The counterparty is a legal system. It requires a named accountable person, signatures, and standing to answer a regulator. No AI can hold that standing.

Hire trigger: First regulator or counsel interaction that recurs; or first consumer-law dispute reaching formal stage; or launch in a second jurisdiction.

R2

Infrastructure / Server Administration

Owns: Hetzner host, deploy ref and rollback, backups and restore proofs, SSL, DNS, secret and key custody, breaker Restore after a T3 vendor ban, disk and cost ceilings.

Why human: The counterparty is physical and financial infrastructure with irreversible failure modes and credentials that must never sit in an agent's hands. Also the one lane where the maintenance agent cannot be its own safety net.

Hire trigger: When David is the single point of failure for a restore -- i.e. before the first period he is unreachable for >24h with live sellers on the platform.

R3

Business & Partner Interface

Owns: Agency contracts and onboarding, Paystack, Travelpayouts, founding-seller relationships, partner escalations, commercial terms.

Why human: The counterparty is another human organisation that negotiates, builds trust over time, and will not transact with a bot on terms.

Hire trigger: Beyond roughly 5-8 active partner organisations, or the first partner who requires a named account contact in contract.

R4

Accountable Principal (David)

Owns: Non-delegable signature: spend above cap, legal sign-off, launch gate, arming autonomy levels, reversing a design ruling.

Why human: Accountability cannot be delegated to a system that cannot be held accountable.

Hire trigger: Never delegated. Deputised only by formal appointment.

6  Pre-design rules

Canon. ai_accountability_check.py fails if any of the eight goes missing. Amber-topped rules are new in v2 and encode the correction.

PR-1

PARALLEL RUN, NEVER A SWITCH-OVER

No mechanism is replaced without both running side by side and compared under real load. The parallel lane is AI-versus-AI or AI-versus-deterministic -- never AI-versus-human, which cannot keep pace and so proves nothing.

PR-2

AI IS POLICED BY AI; HUMANS OWN INTERFACES

A wrong AI answer is caught by a deterministic guard, a second vendor lane, or an economic absorber. Humans are not the fallback for AI tasks -- they own the boundaries where MarketSquare meets the law (R1), the machine (R2), a partner organisation (R3), or must sign (R4).

PR-3

NEVER REMOVE THE FALLBACK AND ADD THE NEW LANE IN THE SAME CHANGE

Yum mandated Dragon Tail AND centralised the DoorDash contract at once, so the franchise lost the AI's replacement and its own tools simultaneously. One change at a time.

PR-4

RUN THE DRAGON TAIL TEST ON EVERY NEW INFORMATION SURFACE

Before exposing any platform-internal signal to sellers, buyers, agencies or affiliates, write down what a purely self-interested actor does with it. Systems modelling, not staffing.

PR-5

NO PUBLIC CLAIM THE MACHINERY CANNOT YET HONOUR

Klarna's most expensive loss was narrative. Extend the vendor-neutral copy discipline (RG-0035) to capability claims.

PR-6

A HUMAN IN A THROUGHPUT PATH IS A DEFECT, NOT A CONTROL

It throttles the business and it decays -- a reviewer at volume rubber-stamps within days, leaving a control that only looks like one. If a design puts a human on a per-item path, the design is wrong. human_seam_check.py fails it.

PR-7

THE EXCEPTION RATE IS THE TRIGGER, NEVER THE VOLUME

A human interface role engages when the exception RATE crosses a threshold, or when the exception CLASS is legal, financial, partner or infrastructural. Rising volume is the design working, and must never pull a human in.

PR-8

THE +1 LANE IS A CONTROL, NOT JUST A SPARE

The designated swap-out provider sits idle in normal operation. On RED surfaces, run it as a second opinion and treat DISAGREEMENT -- not low confidence -- as the exception signal. Dormant failover capacity becomes a live, throughput-preserving control.
