# YouTube — what publishing actually needs (PROBED/READ 6 Sep 2026)

Answers the question "do we have to subscribe, and what else?" so no session re-researches it.

## Cost: none

Publishing on YouTube is free. There is no subscription, no upload fee, no minimum subscriber
count to post. YouTube Premium is a *viewer* product and is irrelevant to uploading. The
Partner Program (1,000 subscribers + 4,000 watch hours, or 10M Shorts views in 90 days) only
governs whether the channel EARNS money — it has nothing to do with the right to publish.

## The three things needed, in order

1. **A Google account** — exists (dmcontiki2@gmail.com).
2. **A YouTube channel** — does NOT exist. This is the only blocker. Creating an account is
   David's act (Claude may not create accounts or enter passwords), and the name/handle/banner
   are launch positioning, which is his under RUL-103(f). A Brand Account is the right shape:
   it can be transferred and can carry extra managers without sharing the Gmail password.
3. **Phone verification** (youtube.com/verify) — free, one SMS code, per channel. Unlocks
   uploads over 15 minutes, live streaming, and custom thumbnails.

## What verification does and does not buy us

- All ten launch films are 42–69 s, so the 15-minute cap never bites. Verification is not
  needed to post them.
- Custom thumbnails DO need verification. Note: custom thumbnails specifically for **Shorts**
  began rolling out to Partner Program channels on 24 Jul 2026 and are desktop-Studio only, so
  a brand-new channel may not get the control at all. The `thumbnail.jpg` in each pack is then
  a nice-to-have; YouTube will use a frame from the film. Not a blocker either way.

## Per-upload obligations (every video, no exceptions)

- **Audience declaration** — "Is this made for kids?" is compulsory (US COPPA). Our films are
  not made for kids; answering wrongly is the one legal trap in the flow.
- **Altered/synthetic content disclosure** — required when realistic content is AI-generated.
  Our films are AI-generated but are plainly illustrative product demos, not realistic depictions
  of real people or events. Declare it anyway on any film with a synthetic human presenter
  (11 · tutor spotlight, when made). Cheap to declare, expensive to be caught not declaring.
- **Paid-promotion checkbox** — not applicable today: every link points to trustsquare.co, our
  own site. It becomes applicable the day an affiliate link (Travelpayouts) appears in a
  description.
- Music/voice in the cuts is ours or licensed — no third-party claim expected. If Content ID
  claims one, the claim shows in Studio at upload, before publishing.

## Automation status

There is no YouTube connector in the MCP registry (searched 6 Sep 2026), so uploads are manual
in Studio: pick a title from the pack's `metadata.md`, upload the mp4, paste description + tags,
answer the audience question, publish, post the pinned comment. About two minutes per film.

- Add custom thumbnails — https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/72431
