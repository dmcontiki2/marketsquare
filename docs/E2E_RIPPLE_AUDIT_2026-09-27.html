What stops the stories working

  1. Every AI Features report fails. The AI Features service on the server has no AI key, so Collectables, Property Dossier, Car Dossier and Liquidation Plan all return "That did not work"; the server log shows no successful report at all.
  2. New sellers cannot receive any introduction. Until a seller uploads ID, introductions are refused — and Quick never tells her this before she publishes.
  3. Local Market lets a buyer fill the whole request form, then refuses it. The refusal is a toast that disappears in about two seconds.
  4. There is no "send my link to my regulars". The only way to connect a customer for Buzz is the "someone you worked for" reference, which is hidden in the AI trust coach and calls the customer her employer.
  5. The reference link dead-ends for a new person. "Please sign in first", with no sign-in button on that page.
  6. Replying to a Buzz email reaches TrustSquare support, not the sender. Your own "Yes" reply today went to support@trustsquare.co.
  7. "Is this a fair price?" is missing where Jacques needs it. It shows only on property (free, whole-city band); none on Collectors or Local Market, while the Wallet still sells it at 1T.

Have Marietjie, Jacques or Kate opened the apps?

  • Marietjie: no sign-in since 19 September; her 500 Tuppence tester credit is untouched.
  • Jacques and Kate: no TrustSquare accounts exist for them.
  • Quick opens can't be seen: the web server's visit log has been empty since 24 September, so opening a page without signing in leaves no trace.
  • What it means: none of them has got as far as signing in yet, so there is nothing yet to show where they got stuck.
