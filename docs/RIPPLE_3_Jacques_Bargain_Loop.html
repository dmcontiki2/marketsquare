Example 3 · value first, then the real auction

Jacques knows what a lot is worth before the hammer falls

Marietjie shares Quick with her brother Jacques. Before each real auction he reads the catalogue and values the lots with TrustSquare's AI features: Collectables + market report (5T) for antiques and jewellery, Property Area Dossier (3T) for property. He walks into the auction room knowing his top bid after the auction's costs, buys only real bargains, and sells them again at a specialist auction or on a free TrustSquare listing, using the same valuation to set his price.

Costs used. Antiques and jewellery: a top-tier house's published rates, 17.5% + VAT buyer's premium and 17.5% + VAT seller's commission (lots up to R100 000). Property: 10% + VAT buyer's commission, about 4% transfer and conveyancing (no transfer duty below R1.21m), and resale through an estate agent at 5% + VAT. A TrustSquare listing charges him nothing; his buyer pays the 1T introduction. The only Tuppence Jacques pays is for the valuations.
