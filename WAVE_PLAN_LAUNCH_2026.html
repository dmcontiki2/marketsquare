⚠ WHAT ACTUALLY HAPPENED, 28 Aug – 1 Sep (measured 1 Sep from the send log + prospects DB): The plan and the reality differ in four material ways, and the plan above has been corrected to the reality. (1) The soft launch did not send. 28 Aug shows ZERO emails; the ladder actually opened on 29 Aug. (2) The one-category-per-day rule did not hold on day one — 29 Aug ran Tutors AND Services in both proving cities (PTA 25+19, JHB 22+20 = 86 against a 60 ceiling). (3) Launch day was smaller and narrower than planned: 57 stamped sends, all Tutors, across six cities that actually got a batch (DBN 11 · CPT 10 · SYD 10 · BFN 9 · EL 8 · PE 7) plus single sends to PLK and NY. Pretoria HELD on its 7.9% stop-loss; Johannesburg had NO tutor pool left; NLP/KIM/PMB skipped on empty pools. Against a 420 planned ceiling, real volume was 57. (4) The 00:10 scheduled task never fired — it was registered at ~04:13, after its own start boundary, so Task Scheduler never triggered it. Both runs that day were started by hand.
The crashed first run, and why the numbers do not reconcile: run 1 (04:19 SAST) died per-city on a cp1252 encoding fault once stdout was redirected to the log — each “successful” city sent exactly ONE real email and then crashed, in send→mark→print order, so no duplicates. Sydney sent zero on that run for a second reason: a launch code containing the segment -R06J false-positived the residual-Rand currency scan and fail-closed the city. Fixed the same morning at class level: PYTHONUTF8=1 across the lane, the arrow glyph replaced, and TSL-CODE-SCAN-1 now masks launch codes before the currency scan. Run 2 (04:32, David’s click) was clean.
COUNT WITH CARE — emailed_at undercounts by 14: the crashed run left rows carrying a sent-class status (emailed / opened / bounced) with NO emailed_at timestamp — 14 of them, including Pretoria 6+1, Sydney 2, London 1, Johannesburg 1 and three bounces. London’s single send is provably real (the row is marked opened) yet carries no timestamp, which is why a date-filtered count shows London at zero. True cumulative sends to date: 184 (either signal). Any figure quoted from emailed_at alone — including the day row above — is a floor, not a total. This is a measurement defect worth fixing before the numbers are used for a gate decision.
✓ GATE CLEARED the same evening — OUTREACH-MARKETS-1 (1 Sep): for a few hours this WAS a launch blocker: the reply agent's FAQ class answered every prospect from hardcoded South African facts, so a New York or London tutor asking “which cities do you cover?” would have been told Johannesburg and Bloemfontein. Fixed. The classifier now IDENTIFIES THE MARKET first — from the city named in the quoted outreach email, the signature, or the sender's domain — and answers only from that market's row (ZA / US / UK / AU open; NZ, NA, AR, FR, PT answered honestly as not open yet). If the market cannot be determined with confidence it falls through to commercial for a human, because a confident answer about the wrong country is worse than a handover. The market rows also distinguish ESTABLISHED (ZA, tutors already listing) from OPENING NOW (New York, London, Sydney — founding tutors, and it must not claim tutors are already listed there): RUL-088's weakest-holder test applied to a market claim. RG-0236 now cross-checks the table against cities.json, so arming a new country without a fact row turns the ledger red. The 4 Sep global roll and A-plan Waves 2–5 are unblocked once this deploys.
Where replies now go (RUL-087, 1 Sep): replies to david@trustsquare.co no longer reach David’s inbox. They are classified on arrival into four classes — machine mail (autoresponders, bounces: silent, logged), FAQ (answered from canon), opt-out (courteous close + suppression, a legal duty), and commercial (calls, terms, agency deals: escalated to David through the admin surface). Machine-silence is live; everything else drafts and queues until the classifier is measured. Auto-send is OFF (OUTREACH_AUTO_SEND unset, verified in the live process env).
No scheduler — each day is a decision: nothing on disk schedules the daily ladder. Every day’s send is David’s act (RUL-053(d)) or a task he orders registered. The 1 Sep task failure is the evidence for treating the dated columns above as INTENT, never as automation that will happen on its own.
Outstanding for Wed 2 Sep: New York’s 20-prospect pool, London’s 16 and Polokwane’s 3 complete when RAMP-1’s minimum gap frees them after their single run-1 sends — note that NY and LDN completing is itself a non-ZA send and therefore sits behind the RG-0236 gate above.
