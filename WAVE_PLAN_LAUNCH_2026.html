Gate 2 (Pretoria complete): formal go/no-go for the fan-out. Gate 3 (all SA-city ladders complete): unlocks agency waves — your filling-portfolio logic; the agency email template literally shows live listings from the prospect's own city.
Deliverability ramp riding this plan: volumes grow city-by-city, never as one blast — this is what keeps trustsquare.co out of junk folders. Weekends stay in the ladder deliberately: B&B hosts and private sellers read email on weekends; corporates don't, and corporates come in Phase 3 on weekdays.
