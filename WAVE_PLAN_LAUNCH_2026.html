Volume cap: 30 emails per category per city per day (RUL-053). With Johannesburg proving alongside Pretoria the worst-case curve is 60/day for the four warm-up days, the same 420 peak on launch day, then a 270–360 plateau tailing to 90. The 1–2 Sep totals are UNCHANGED — Johannesburg only moves 30/day out of the lagged block into Phase 1 — and 3–6 Sep each drop by 30 because two cities now finish on 2 Sep instead of one. The ramp is a gentler 60 → 420 rather than 30 → 420.
