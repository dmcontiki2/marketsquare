My suggestions, one line each

Keep it to one screen with three tabs — People, Send, Specials — nothing else.
Adding someone means sharing her own Buzz link, which is how joining already works and keeps phone numbers out of the app.
Edit is only a nickname and a tag, because tags are what make group sends useful.
Remove closes the connection with a 10-second undo, and the customer can always mute or leave on their side.
Ready-made quick lines cover most sends, so "I'll be late" is two taps: pick the line, press Send.
A "Today's bookings" group sends a late message only to the people actually affected, not to everyone.
A group send is many separate one-to-one buzzes, so there is no group chat to moderate and nobody sees the other customers.
A special is saved on the listing with an end date and the buzz just says what it is and "tap to see".
Keep the 120-character limit the server already enforces.
Limit sends to everyone to about one a day and three specials a week, so her customers don't feel spammed and mute her.

Decided — David, 17 Oct 2026

Free: one-to-one buzzes, plus the ready-made service lines (on my way, late, can't make it, done) to a group or everyone.

Paid (Starter and Pro): Specials, and your own words to a group; on Free these show greyed, and tapping says "Needs a subscription".

No cost per message: Buzz never uses SMS, and a group send goes by push only; the one-to-one email backup stays inside the flat email plan already paid, under a daily ceiling.
