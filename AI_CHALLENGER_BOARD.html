1  Answering the three questions

1. Is OpenAI substantially lower?  —  Yes, and it is also better.

gpt-5.6-luna is 77–79% cheaper than the baseline on the haiku, vision and triage tiers and scores 51.24 against Haiku’s 29.58. It even outscores claude-sonnet-4-6 (47.21) at roughly a thirteenth of the price. It passed the golden set on 1 Aug 2026 — 8/8 on production prompts including a two-photo real case.

2. Are the Scaleway options substantially higher capability?  —  No — but with one caveat in your favour.

mistral-medium-3.5-128b scores 29.95 against Haiku’s 29.58 — a 1.25% edge, not a substantial one — while costing 73% more on three of four tiers. As a value lane it is worse than the baseline.

The caveat matters though: Haiku’s score carries effort_matched: false — it was benchmarked in reasoning mode while the app runs default — whereas Mistral’s is effort_matched: true. So Haiku’s 29.58 is flattered relative to how it actually runs, and Mistral’s modest edge is probably a real one. Any decision here must re-score at production effort, not trust the leaderboard.

And note what the ONE-MODEL row does: one model across four tiers means Scaleway is 1.73× dearer on haiku/vision/triage and a 37% capability drop on sonnet (29.95 vs 47.21). The card’s own words: “CONTINUITY/JURISDICTION standby, not a cheap lane.” It is EU insurance, priced as insurance. It should never be judged as value.

3. Does Anthropic stay there even after upgrades?  —  Yes. Structurally.

Not by anyone’s decision — by the composition of three individually sensible rules. See section 3.

3  Why the incumbent cannot currently be displaced

Each of these is defensible on its own. Anti-jitter prevents thrash; not re-auditioning sitting models saves money; advisory-not-automatic respects the ruling. Together they compose into permanent incumbency — an outcome none of them intended.

The absolute switch floor is formally UNMET

min_net_saving_usd_90d: 50 needs spend-log volume that does not exist yet. A floor that cannot be computed cannot be cleared.

Sitting models are never re-auditioned

Funnel step 4: “sitting models are not re-auditioned continuously.” The incumbent never takes the test, so it can never fail it.

The cheapest, highest-scoring lane cannot enter

openai is absent from GOLDEN_PASS in ai_scoreboard.py by design, pending a server-key golden run. It is excluded from the ranking however cheap or capable it is.

The only cost ranking is discarded

bea_main.py:17900 calls ai_scoreboard.run_nightly() and throws the return value away. Nothing reads it, and it is spend-gated off by default.

The fix, consistent with the ruling

The ruling forbids automating the switch. It does not forbid automating the challenge. Separate them: the board runs on a 30-day cadence and surfaces the case; the incumbent is re-auditioned every 90 days at its production effort level; a lane marked BLOCKED BY PROCESS carries a named owner and a date. The decision stays yours — but silence stops being one of the answers.
