Found something wrong? Tell us.

You are using TrustSquare before anyone else, which means you will hit things that are broken. That is not an inconvenience to us — it is the entire point. Here is how to report one, and what happens afterwards.

Look at the right-hand edge of any page. This tab follows you everywhere in the app — the marketplace, your listings, the adventure maps, the terms pages. Tap it the moment something misbehaves, while you are still on the screen that went wrong.

REPORT

What we ask you

  • What went wrong — one line is enough.
  • What you were doing, and what you expected — the steps you took, and what happened instead.
  • How badly it affected you — blocked me completely / got in my way / small or cosmetic.
  • Which part of the app — pick the closest; "not sure" is a perfectly good answer.
  • Your email — so we can tell you when it is fixed.
  • A screenshot, if you have one. Optional, and often worth more than a paragraph.
You do not need to describe your phone or the page you were on. The form quietly picks up the page address, the app version, your screen size, your browser and the last few technical errors it recorded. That is usually what lets us reproduce the fault without having to ask you anything.

What happens next

You

You send it

The moment you tap send, it is logged with a reference like TS-0007.

Us

You get an email within seconds

Confirming the reference and that it is in the fix queue.

Nothing further is needed from you. If a fix needs something only you can tell us, we will write to that address.

Us

We fix it — worst first

Anything that blocked you completely jumps the queue. Everything else is ordered behind it.

If two of you report the same thing, it stays one fault with two voices behind it — which makes it more likely to be fixed sooner, not less.

Us

We write to you and say what changed

Not "resolved". The actual change we made, quoting what you originally reported.

You

You check it, and that is what closes it

Open the app, repeat the steps that failed, and tell us whether it now behaves.

If it is still wrong, report it again from the same tab and quote the reference — it stays on the same thread. A fault is only marked confirmed when you say so. We do not get to mark our own homework.

What makes a report easy to fix

Say what you did, not what you think is broken

"Tapped Publish on my listing, the spinner span for about a minute, then the page went back to the form and my photos were gone."

"The publish function is broken."

The first one we can reproduce in two minutes. The second sends us hunting. Neither is wrong to send — send the second if the first is too much effort, always.

A word on the screenshots. A screenshot captures whatever was on your screen — please glance at it before attaching. And a small point of honesty: we store your report, your email and those details so we can fix the fault and reply to you. Nothing else, and nobody else.
