Why we must register

The Data Protection (Registration of Data Controllers and Data Processors) Regulations, 2021 exempt small businesses (turnover under KES 5 million and fewer than 10 staff) — except those processing data for the purposes in the Third Schedule. Two of them are ours: “hospitality industry firms” (Adventures accommodation) and “property management including the selling of land” (Property listings). The ODPC also says a controller not established in Kenya that processes data of people in Kenya must register.

The steps

  1. Open dataportal.odpc.go.ke → Register (Data Handler Registration) and create the account with the company's email.
  2. Choose Data Controller (TrustSquare decides why and how the data is used).
  3. Fill the application (Form DPR1) with the answers in the table below.
  4. Upload the company's establishment document: the CIPC registration certificate of TrustSquare (Pty) Ltd. you
  5. Pay KES 4,000 (M-Pesa, card, bank transfer RTGS or cheque; M-Pesa and card add KES 50).
  6. Watch the company email: the certificate comes in about 14 days if nothing more is asked. Put the renewal date (24 months) in the calendar.
