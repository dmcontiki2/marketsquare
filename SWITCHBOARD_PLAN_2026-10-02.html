The Switchboard Plan

How TrustSquare's AI models and agents become plug-ins: cheap, swappable, European, with Claude kept out of the app's way.

Prepared 2 Oct 2026 for the Fable 5.1 session after the weekly usage reset. The facts below were checked in the code and files today.

1 · The one big idea

The new Mod runs inside Claude Code on your PC. That makes it a great cockpit, but the wrong place for the live app's brain. If the app's AI ran through Claude Code, the app would stop whenever your PC or Claude Code was closed. Claude would also sit in the middle of every customer call, which is the conflict of interest you want to avoid.

So the plan splits the work into three layers:

Cockpit (your view)Switchboard (server)ModelsAgentsClaude (a tool, not the brain)

3 · The model ladder for every job

Each job (fast replies, reasoning, reading photos and so on) gets its own ladder. The Switchboard always starts at the top and only steps down when something goes wrong.

1
The cheapest model that passes the test
Never Claude. Today this is OpenAI Luna / Terra.
2
The next cheapest, from a different company
One company's outage can't take out both rungs.
3
An open model hosted in Europe
Mistral / Qwen on Scaleway or OVH. The data stays in the EU.
4
Claude, as the last resort
Used only when rungs 1–3 are all down. An alarm rings, and it is time-limited.
What makes it step down (automatically, in seconds)
🔴 Model down🟠 Throttled (too many requests)🐢 Too slow🔒 Key blocked💸 Daily cost cap close

What needs you (one tap)
⬆ Promote a new, cheaper model to rung 1↩ Return to the normal model after an outage

Stepping down is automatic. Stepping back up is your call, because of your 14 Aug ruling: a silent model change can move profit from good to very bad.

New: the weekly Model Scout. Once a week it reads the price lists of every vendor we have connected. It tests any new cheap model on our golden test set for each job. Then it puts a card in your cockpit, for example: "Gemini 3.7 Flash passes the reason job at one third of today's cost. Promote?" You tap yes or no. The Scout only suggests. It never switches anything by itself.

4 · Agents as plug-ins, each with a passport

Every agent plugs into the same socket and carries the same five things. One screen can then show them all, and any agent's brain can be swapped without touching the agent.

Passport fieldWhat it says
🧠 BrainWhich job ladder it uses. It never names a model directly.
🔑 PermissionsWhat it may read, write, send or deploy (the host-queue allowlist, per agent)
💰 Cost capIts own daily spending limit. It stops cleanly when it reaches it.
⏰ ScheduleWhen it runs. Runs happen on the server, never in a paid chat session.
❤️ HealthA check the cockpit reads: last run, last result, red / green / grey

QA Bot

The independent auditor. Its brain stays OpenAI on purpose, because Claude must never audit Claude.

Maintenance agent

Watches the system and repairs faults. Its brain comes from the Switchboard ladder.

BIT self-test

The built-in test, kept separate from the app it tests. It needs no AI.

CityLauncher

Finds and invites sellers in each city. Sending stays inside the approved waves.

AdvertAgent

Writes and checks adverts. Uses the cheap text ladder.

SellerScraper

Supply only: it gathers prospects and sends nothing.

MinutesMaker

Turns meeting recordings into minutes. Uses the speech and text ladders.

Genie (in-app helper)

Talks to customers. Uses the fast ladder, with a strict cost cap per user.

5 · The Cockpit Mod: what you would see

Band above the prompt

One line that is always visible: Ledger 🟢 · AI today R41 · OpenAI serving · 0 alarms

Switchboard pane

Every job's ladder, which rung is serving, today's cost against the cap, and the last failover, all colour-coded.

Agent pane

Every agent's passport on one screen, in red / green / grey, with its last run time.

One-tap buttons

Promote a Scout pick, run a failover drill, or request a deploy. Each one is a permission request, not a job for you to click through.

Rules become rails ★

The Mod can block an action before it happens. The CLAUDE.md rules that keep getting broken (no Edit/Write on big project files, no direct copies to the server, no Claude-first model) stop being reminders and become walls.

Alerts as pop-ups

A small pop-up appears while you work when a failover happens or a cost cap gets close.

★ = my addition to your brief. Being able to stop an action before it runs is the Mod's most useful power for this project.

6 · Servers: Europe first, a spare elsewhere

Why a different company in Step 2: a second Hetzner box in Helsinki would not help if Hetzner itself blocks or loses the account. Why Johannesburg in Step 3: your users are in South Africa, and every page currently travels to Germany and back. A local spare also makes the app feel quicker.

8 · The Fable session road map

It runs as one Goal, the method you used for onboarding on 5 Sep. Fable gets the goal file, works through the phases, and proves each finish line itself.

PHASE 6

Quick & fluid pass

Turn the speed targets into ledger checks, split ms.js and polish the design.

Done when: the four targets above are measured on the live site.

9 · Only two things are yours to decide

MONEY
The Paris standby server (Step 2), and later Johannesburg (Step 3). Each has a small monthly cost. Fable designs and prices it, then it waits for your yes.
MODELS
Each time a new model is promoted to rung 1. The Scout shows the full field and the test scores, and you tap yes or no in the cockpit.

Everything else is technical, and Fable decides it from the rulings and canon files. Your rule "Claude is never the first option" is now written into the rulings register (RUL-195), so it can't drift.
