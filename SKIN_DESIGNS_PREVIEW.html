Tutor institution

DESIGN PREVIEW — not wired · RG-0169 OPEN
Gate (proposed, registry-verified 23 Aug 2026): SACE registration (embeds SAPS clearance + Child Protection Register + Sexual Offenders Register) — or SAQA-verified qualification + the same three clearances.
Child safety IS the gate. SACE will not register an educator without police clearance, and screens the CPR and NRSO; since 2019 schools are legally required to check both registers for anyone working with children. Non-SACE tutors follow the same three clearances with a SAQA-verified qualification.
Also scored: Subjects & NQF level · years · results track record (declared) · the institution's own DHET/Umalusi/CIPC standing

Service company

DESIGN PREVIEW — not wired · RG-0169 OPEN
Gate (proposed, registry-verified 23 Aug 2026): Trade-statutory licence per service class — PIRB-licensed plumber (CoC-issuing) · DoEL Installation Electrician / Wireman's licence · class-keyed table; CIPC for the company.
The gate follows the trade: only PIRB-licensed plumbers and DoEL-licensed installation electricians may issue Certificates of Compliance — the exact credential a homeowner needs honoured. Non-regulated trades fall back to CIPC + insurance, flagged as a weaker gate.
Also scored: Public liability insurance · workmanship guarantees · years · CIDB grading (construction) · response-time commitment

Placement agency

DESIGN PREVIEW — not wired · RG-0169 OPEN
Gate (proposed, registry-verified 23 Aug 2026): DEL private employment agency registration (Employment Services Act 4 of 2014) + CIPC.
Statutory: every private employment agency must register with the Department of Employment and Labour — and the Act PROHIBITS charging workseekers, which is exactly the TrustSquare model: the candidate pays nothing; the consultant accepts the introduction at 1T. Engine stays DARK until studywork_live flips (RUL-046).
Also scored: APSO membership · sponsor networks (IAPA, US J-1 sponsors, cruise lines) · bonding/insurance · years · placements (declared)
