Baseline Readiness

Designing redundancy, independence and accountability into TrustSquare

Claude's input to the Council discussion · 14 September 2026 · discussion paper, not a decision

The one sentence. Every instrument we built measured our own machinery rather than a stranger's experience — a test our code passes proves the code ran, it never proves a stranger succeeded, and that is why a green board and a blocked launch were both true at the same time.

▍ The three words, made testable

They are not three names for reliability. They answer three different questions, and the project fails each one in a different way.

Redundancy second path

Is there a second path, and has it been driven?

Today
One outreach category with five nights of supply, one server, one sending provider, one payment provider, one deploy lane, one founder.
Proposed test
Every load-bearing capability has a named second path, a drill and a recorded drill result — undrilled counts as absent.
Independence second opinion

Does the second opinion share any input with the first?

Today
Our documents were green all through the bad launch, so an auditor reading them inherits the blind spot exactly.
Proposed test
The auditor forms its verdict from outside evidence only — live site, fresh account, public interface — and writes its own test data.
Accountability a name and a cost

Whose name is on the verdict, and what happens when it is wrong?

Today
The reports that said “ready” carried no author, no evidence date and no consequence.
Proposed test
Every verdict names who, what evidence and when — and a blocker after a green verdict fixes the instrument that missed it, same session.

▍ A proposed gate — what “baseline ready” would have to mean

Six conditions. The common property is that none of them can be satisfied by our own machinery agreeing with itself.

#ConditionTodayWhy
1A stranger completes the journey end to end on the live site, with data we did not seed, and is not us.FAILSZero real signups from 1,671 emails
2Every single point of failure has a named second path, drilled within an agreed window.FAILSNo register of them exists yet
3The independent auditor returns green without reading our documents.UNTESTEDThe auditor has always read our documents
4The adversarial pass finds nothing unmitigated above an agreed severity.UNTESTEDNo adversarial pass is defined
5Every instrument that must fire has been proven to fire against a deliberately broken case.PARTLYDone on a few checks, never required
6No number on the readiness board counts our own test data.PASSESFixed today — this was the six-week lie
★ The cheapest change in this paper is condition 5. It already exists in the project's own practice — one check was deliberately broken first and proven red before it was believed green. Making that universal would have caught most of the eight findings above.
