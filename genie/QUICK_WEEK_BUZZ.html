The channel, corrected An earlier draft of this page said push was dead and promised an SMS fallback. Both were wrong. RUL-122 rules SMS out — R0.15–R0.30 a message is a per-message cost, and nothing enters the path unless it is free at the margin. And push is live: the service-worker fault was fixed on 12 September, the VAPID keys are self-hosted, and _push_to_seller() already delivers to every device a user has registered — its title and body are exactly the sender's name and the one line. So the Buzz needs no delivery machinery: only the pair record, the two onboarding switches, and one endpoint. Email is the backup when a phone has no push. WhatsApp stays out for the same reason as SMS.
