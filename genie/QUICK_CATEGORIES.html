Quick Listing — what differs per category, and what never does

RUL-132 in one picture. Every category asks four different questions in a different shape, and every category shares one consent model. Vary the flow freely; never fork the consent. Ten flows are ten things to maintain — two consent models are a hole somebody falls through.

The five things that are identical in all eight
  • A buzz only ever travels between two parties who are already connected — a stranger can never appear.
  • Each side holds its own switch, and consent is never transitive.
  • The sender's name is on every buzz and on every answer.
  • One close ends it both ways, the other person is told without blame, and only the closer reopens.
  • Push first, email as the backup, and never a per-message cost.
That list is the whole of what may not vary. Everything above it is free to differ, and should.
