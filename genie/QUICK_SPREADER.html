Your requirement and the ruling are the same thing. "Separate app" has two meanings and only one of them was ever ruled on. RUL-125(a) chose a separate APP — its own link, its own name, its own colour, its own icon, and the TrustSquare interface never appears — while keeping one ORIGIN, so the sign-in, the push subscription and the service worker are shared for free. trustsquare.co/quick/ is the link you put in every email instead of the app link. He wants the shelf, introductions and Tuppence, none of which Quick has. This is the half of your sentence that says the link "will link to the trustsquare app to then create an icon" — and it is the hirer, not the lister, for whom that is true.

Why two icons and not one One icon forces a choice that cannot be right for both: give everyone the Quick tile and the hirer has no route to the marketplace he actually wants; give everyone the full app and you have thrown away the lightness that made the spreader spread. Two tiles, two moments, one account behind both — and because it is one origin, the second install costs the user no second sign-in and no second permission.
