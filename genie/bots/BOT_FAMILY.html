Why the app’s own colours cannot be the icons

The seven tile colours in BRAND_ASSETS.md are right where they are: they sit behind a photograph inside the app, so they are deliberately dark and recessive. Put the same seven on a home screen as icons and they stop being seven things.

Measured with CIEDE2000 — the standard for how different two colours look to a human eye. Under about 20 is too close for two icons; under 10 is the same colour to the eye.

Three of them are green — Tutors, Adventures and Local Market. Tutors and Local Market are ΔE 6 apart, which is not a difference anybody will see on a 60-pixel icon. And every one of the seven is dark: lightness runs from 9 to 37 out of 100, so on a dark home screen they are seven dark squares.

The palette that does work

Nothing new was invented. Each BOT keeps the hue its category already owns — taken from the pastel set you picked for the explainer video, which had already separated blue, green, peach, teal, sand, periwinkle and pink. They are pulled up to icon strength: real chroma, mid lightness, and the crowded ones pushed apart until every pair clears.

The app tiles do not change. This is a second, sibling palette for icons and BOT screens only — same hue, different job.

The rule, in three lines

HueComes from the category, and the category list is canon. Nobody picks a BOT colour by taste.
ShadeBelongs to the individual BOT. A trade inside a category takes a lighter or deeper shade of its family — never a hue of its own.
RingA sub-trade BOT wears its family hue as a thin ring, so Home Help reads as a Services BOT at a glance even in clay.

Which is what keeps this from becoming a colour argument every time a BOT is added. A plumbing BOT is a deeper rust. A maths-tutor BOT is a deeper green. Neither needs a decision.

What actually ships, per BOT

A BOT is one manifest file plus three icons pointing at the same app — same code, same server, same login, same Trust Score. The eight manifests are written and sitting in genie/bots/. Nothing is deployed and nothing is in the deploy manifest.

Still to make when any of this is built: three PNG icons per BOT in its colour, under /static/brand/bots/<bot>/. That is a drawing job, not a decision.
