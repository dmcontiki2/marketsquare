How an advert gets its second language

1

She writes in her own language

The Quick door already picks it from her phone, and the advert stores it as its original language.

2

She picks one extra language

Only from the languages you approved for her country.

3

The app drafts it and shows her a check

Next to the draft she sees it translated back into her own language, so she can approve what it really says.

4

Nothing goes live until she approves

The original publishes straight away; the extra language waits for her tap.

5

Every advert is findable in English

An English search layer sits behind each advert, so an isiZulu-only advert still turns up for an English search.

6

The buyer sees his language if it's approved

Otherwise he sees the original, with the code chip telling him which language it is in.

If she edits the original, the extra language drops back to draft and asks her again, so the two never drift apart.
