Settled

David, 4 September 2026: the helper shape is what we build, the genie may not cover any other selector, and it waits for a stable user base.

The genie is not a new search. It is the front door and the voice of the narrowing funnel we already ratified on 30 August (the Zoom spec, ZOOM_HMI_SPEC.md). That funnel already asks one question at a time, already computes true counts, already removes dead ends. The genie adds exactly two things to it: an opening circle of the seven categories for a person who has not chosen one yet, and a face on the question.

Written up as section 11 of the Zoom spec. It is not on the roadmap and not in a build window — it is parked behind the trigger below.

The covering rule

David's words: the genie should not cover any of the other selectors. Made concrete:

  • The genie band is a row, not a sheet. It takes its own space and the results shrink above it. Nothing slides over anything.
  • The search field, the category chips, the filter, sort and trust controls and the bottom bar stay visible and working at every step. Try it: open the genie, then tap a category chip or the filter — they still act.
  • One full-screen moment is allowed in the whole flow: the opening circle of seven. It is allowed because nothing has been selected yet, so there is nothing to cover.
  • The floating orb parks in the gutter above the bottom bar, where no control lives, and hides itself whenever the band or a panel is open.

Shape B breaks every one of these, which is what the red strip is reporting.

What was kept, what was cut

David's originalCallWhy
Floating genie you tap to startKept One friendly door into search, and the charm of the whole idea.
Circle of the 7 categoriesKept Seven fits a circle, and it is the one moment a picture beats a word. It is also the step the funnel does not have today — the funnel starts inside a category.
Circles at every level below thatCut A circle holds seven. Tutors have eight subjects and Services have dozens — the labels already touch in shape B. Below the top level: chips, which wrap and scroll.
Real 3D iconsCut The look is kept and the technology is not. Every ball here is plain CSS. Real 3D means an artist drawing a new model each time we add a subject.
Ask city, then suburbCut The app knows the city. The funnel already handles suburb properly — it never opens with geography, and it goes only as deep as the category deserves.
Search available at any momentKept Better than kept: in shape A the results are never hidden, so there is no search button to go back to.
Deeper than level — openings, endgame, speed chessKept, automatic The funnel already decides this by itself: it only asks a question that actually splits the remaining set, and it skips one that has a single answer. So the chess depth appears on its own when there are enough chess tutors, and stays quiet when there are not.

When we look at it again

Parked deliberately. The genie's whole value is the counts on those seven balls — it is a window into the shop. A ring showing 0 on four of seven categories advertises an empty shop to the first person who taps it. So the trigger is stock, not calendar:

1
The narrowing funnel itself is built and switched on in the field. That is David's act, and the genie has nothing to sit on until it happens.
2
All seven categories show a real count in a typical user's city.
3
Roughly a month of live search behaviour to compare against — so we can tell whether the genie helped or merely looked good.

Cost when it comes back: small, and only if it is built on the funnel — an opening screen, a persona and the covering rule, on machinery that already exists. Built as its own path it is a second search engine to keep in step with the first one forever, and every future filter has to be built twice. That is the one line that must not be forgotten between now and then.
