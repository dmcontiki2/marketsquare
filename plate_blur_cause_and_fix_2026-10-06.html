Number-plate blur — why it was a blob, and what runs now

Before — one model asked to do two jobs

1. Vision LLM names coordinates (0–1000) — reads well, measures badly: 5–10% of the frame off, sometimes below the plate.
2. Code pads the box because it cannot trust it.
3. LLM re-checks, says "still readable" → another imprecise box painted on top, up to 4 rounds.
4. Last-resort rung expands every box ever drawn by 30–60%. = the blob.
Since 19 Aug (RUL-033) the blur was switched off entirely → a car photo with a plate was rejected: "not anonymous — replace it". That was today's 15:45 experience (2 of 5 Ford photos refused).

Now — each model does its own job

0. Local plate detector (RT-DETRv2, Apache-2.0, ONNX on the Hetzner box, ~0.8 s, R0 per photo) returns the plate to the pixel.
0b. Plate-shaped blur: plate + frame margin for the dealer strip, soft 2 px edge, nothing else touched.
1. LLM scan on the already-blurred photo: signage, logos, the seller's own label, inappropriate content, category fit — and the final "is it clean" read. Normally "clean" first time.
2. If the LLM still names a plate, the detector's box replaces its guess, one verify read, done. Non-plate items follow the old path unchanged.
Eval: 11/11 plates found (incl. the tiny background one), 0 false boxes on real clean photos. Fail-safe: no model → yesterday's behaviour.
