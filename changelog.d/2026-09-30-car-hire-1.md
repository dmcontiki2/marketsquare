- **CAR-HIRE-1 (RG-0574), from the F9 story walk (vehicle for hire):** TrustSquare's Sell never asked whether a car is for sale or for hire, so a hire car could only be set in Edit afterwards, and its advert still said a plain "Price" with nothing about the hire. Sell now asks "For sale or for hire?". For hire, the price is a rate per day and the Condition step becomes **Hire terms**: deposit, shortest hire, free kilometres a day, who may drive, and other terms. They show on the advert, and the buyer sees "For hire · hire rate" above the price. A hire car is not offered the Car Purchase Dossier.
- **CAR-HIRE-1 follow-up:** Sell's last step, "The legal side", showed a car for hire the paperwork for *selling* a car (change of ownership, the RC1, the buyer registering within 21 days). A hire car now skips that step and goes straight to its score; the sale card stays for cars for sale.
