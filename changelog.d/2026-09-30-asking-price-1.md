- **ASKING-PRICE-1 (RG-0559), from the F6 story walk (house for sale):** Quick's door promises "a few taps and your price", but the Property door never asked one — a house went live with no price, and a buyer's "Is this a fair price?" had nothing to check. After the bedrooms Quick now asks "What is your asking price?" (or, to let, "What is the rent a month?"). She types it the way she says it — "2 450 000", "2.45m" or "13 990" — and it is saved as "R2 450 000" or "R13 990 / month". In all five languages.
