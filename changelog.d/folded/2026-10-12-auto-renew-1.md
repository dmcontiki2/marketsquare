**What was built**
- **Paystack plans, made once, by maintenance — never by a checkout.** `_auto_renew_sync_plans()` makes (or adopts, if it
  already exists on the Paystack account) one monthly ZAR plan per product — Starter R90, Pro R360, Agency Pro seat R90 —
  and stores each code in `paystack_plans` against the key mode it was made in (a test-key plan is never used with the
  live key). It runs from `scripts/paystack_plans_sync.py` (through the admin route `POST /admin/auto-renew/plans`, so the
  Paystack key never leaves the running app), from the arming of the switch, and hourly while the switch is on and a plan
  is missing. Amounts come from `_SELLER_SUB_TIERS`, never typed twice.
- **Checkout.** `/payment/seller-subscription/initialize` takes two optional parameters, `auto_renew` and `channel`. A plan
  code is sent to Paystack only when ALL of these hold: the tick box was left on, the payment is by card, the switch is
  on, the tier's plan exists. Otherwise the original one-off call runs, untouched.
- **Webhook** (`/payment/webhook`, signature check unchanged): `subscription.create` stores the subscription code and
  email token; a renewal `charge.success` (one carrying one of our plans) extends `billing_period_end` by 30 days from
  the later of the previous end and the payment, grants the month's Tuppence (once a month, by `grant_monthly_tuppence`'s
  own key), clears a failed-renewal mark, and is applied once per reference; `invoice.payment_failed` sets
  `renewal_failed_at` and sends ONE Buzz (the free web-push lane) + ONE email; `subscription.disable` /
  `subscription.not_renew` set `auto_renew=0` and nothing else. The first payment's own `charge.success` is also applied
  from the webhook (same door and once-only claim as `/verify`), so a seller who closes the tab at Paystack still gets
  the plan she is being billed for.
- **Cancel renewal:** `POST /payment/seller-subscription/cancel` (and `/resume` for "Renew again") — declared in
  `route_policy.json` as user routes bound to the session email (SEC-GATE-1). It answers "cancelled" only after Paystack
  confirmed the stop; if Paystack cannot be reached nothing changes and she is told so.
- **The lapse pass (RUL-201)** is unchanged for a seller with no subscription. A plan whose renewal is due is held up to
  72 hours past its end date (Paystack bills on the same day each month, which can be a day after a 30-day period ends);
  a failed renewal ends that wait at once. When such a plan ends, the subscription is switched off at Paystack so the
  card is not tried again a month later, and she is told once.
- **Nobody is billed for two plans:** buying a different plan, moving to Free, an ops move to a free plan, or closing the
  account switches the old subscription off (queued and retried hourly if Paystack is unreachable).
- **Schema** (ALTER TABLE in `run_migrations`, like `listings.reach`): `users.auto_renew INTEGER DEFAULT 1`,
  `users.paystack_subscription_code`, `users.paystack_email_token`, `users.renewal_failed_at`;
  `launch_switches.auto_renew INTEGER NOT NULL DEFAULT 0`; tables `paystack_plans`, `subscription_events`,
  `paystack_stop_queue`. The code and token never leave the server (`GET /users/{email}` strips all four columns).
- **App (ms.js):** the checkout step with the tick box — ticked by default — "Renew automatically each month — cancel any
  time with one tap.", and under it "Renews automatically every month until you cancel. Cancelling takes one tap and your
  paid days still run to the end." It is on all four plan checkout surfaces: the Plans screen, the Subscription screen,
  the Wallet (My Space → Billing) and seller onboarding. Choosing "EFT or another way" shows the tick box disabled with
  "Automatic renewal needs a card payment" and runs the one-off payment. The subscriber's plan card shows "Renews on
  <date>" + **Cancel renewal** (one tap); after cancelling, "Runs to <date>, then Free" + **Renew again**; after a failed
  renewal, the reason + **Pay again**. `DEMO_MODE` guard on every new call. `marketsquare.html` and `quick.html` are
  untouched (the static plan cards get their line and their checkout step from ms.js; Quick does not sell plans).
- **Dashboard (`dashboard.server.html`):** the +1 page switch "Automatic plan renewal" (never locked by launch mode) and
  four tiles — renewals this month, failed renewals, renewals cancelled, auto-renew share.
- **Never** a `charge_authorization` call: Paystack's subscription does the charging; this server reads events and
  switches a subscription off or back on.

**Decisions taken while building (CTO lane, RUL-037) — David may veto any of them**
1. Paystack bills monthly on the same calendar day, not every 30 days. A renewal therefore runs from the later of the
   previous end and the payment, plus 30 days — nobody loses a paid day — and a plan whose renewal is due waits up to
   72 hours instead of ending a day early.
2. Paystack's documentation says a failed subscription charge is not retried before the next payment date. So after a
   failed renewal the plan ends on its date (RUL-201) and the subscription is switched off, rather than her card being
   tried again a month later. She gets one Buzz + one email in total, not one at the failure and another at the end.
3. Cancel renewal is one tap with no "are you sure" (the ruling says one tap); "Renew again" undoes it.
4. Switching the switch off again stops NEW renewals only. A seller who already renews keeps renewing and can cancel.
5. The Agency Pro seat has no checkout of its own (RG-0167, open since 26 Sep), so there is nothing to put the tick box
   on; its Paystack plan is prepared so the seat inherits it the day that checkout exists.
