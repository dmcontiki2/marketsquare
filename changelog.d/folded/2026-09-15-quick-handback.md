## QUICK-HANDBACK-1 — the seam between the four taps and the long sell path

David, on the same day the Quick door went up: *"We still need the long sell path where more
options and details can be added?"*

**Yes, and nothing about it changed.** Quick composes a thin card from four taps — title, price,
category, city, suburb, description and a handful of category fields — and the server stores every
one of them as `draft` whatever is sent. Photos, the full detail set, the score and the act of
publishing all belong to the guided flow, and always did. The two are not alternatives; Quick is
the door and the guided flow is the room.

**The seam already existed and nobody had said so out loud.** `GET /listings/mine?email=` matches
on `seller_email`, which is exactly what the hand-over now writes, so a Quick draft is already
waiting in the guided flow the moment that person signs in with the same address.

**So the address is the only thing he can get wrong,** and the result box now says so and gives
him the way back: the email in bold, *"the draft is waiting on that address, and only that one"*,
and a link into the app. It used to read "Finish onboarding there to go live" with no door and no
mention of which address — true, and useless to somebody standing outside.

Rendered-verified at 400px on both paths — member and stranger — before shipping.
