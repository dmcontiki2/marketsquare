## 2026-09-14 — EULA v1.16: the Buzz clause goes in while the platform is still empty (RUL-133)

Buzz was built and its clause was a draft parked on the counsel track, so the feature could not
ship without running against terms that did not describe it. David's call: put it in now, because
with no onboarded listers there is no acceptance to re-obtain, no migration and no notice period —
the window closes with the first lister.

- **SS3.8 Buzz — A Convenience, Not a Communications Service.** Not a guarantee of delivery (push,
  falling back to email; telephone if it matters); the Platform does not read, moderate or mediate,
  with SS10.1 and SS3.3 still applying; consent is receiver-held, never transitive, closable by
  either side both ways and reopenable only by whoever closed it; fair-use limits on length and
  number are operational; and a liability limit that expressly preserves gross negligence,
  intentional wrongdoing and anything the CPA does not allow to be excluded.
- **SS9.4 gains a 90-day Buzz delivery-record line**, matching `BUZZ_LOG_KEEP_DAYS` in the code.
- **Three things could not be written as asked** and were corrected in the drafting: *voetstoots*
  is a sale-of-goods term and CPA s55/s56 give a non-excludable right to services of reasonable
  quality; "no complaints allowed" cannot be contracted for at all; and CPA s49 needs the limit
  conspicuous and acknowledged, which is why it sits on screen at the switch.
- **The on-screen copy now names SS3.8 and links to /terms** (`ms.js` `buzzRender`), so the
  acknowledgement at the switch is real rather than nominal.
- **Header drift repaired:** the document header still read "Version 1.14 · 20 August 2026" while
  the footer read v1.15 — `eula_sync.py` compares the three copies to each other, never the header
  to the footer. Both now read v1.16 · 14 September 2026.
- **RG-0367 LOCKED** — five legs: the clause is in the source; in both synced copies; the switch
  still names and links it; the EULA's retention equals the code's constant; and `canon.yml` is at
