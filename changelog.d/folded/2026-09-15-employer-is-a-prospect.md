## RUL-137 - the person who confirms you is somebody we want, not somebody to reassure

David, reading the line "they tap Yes. No account, nothing to sign": *"the whole purpose is to
actually get referrals with the people opening a free account, even if they never use it. I
would not add 'No account' there?"*

He is right, and it was the same instinct twice. The confirm page had been written to strip out
every reason to hesitate - and one of the things it stripped out was the reason to stay. That
page is the cheapest acquisition we have: a real person, arriving because somebody they actually
worked with sent them, already looking at what TrustSquare does for that person.

**Nothing on any surface sells the absence of an account any more.** Not the page, not the
WhatsApp message the seller sends, not the coach's own words.

**The confirmation stays one tap.** It carries no condition and asks for no identity - that is
what makes it honest, and it is why the evidence is worth 12 points. The free account is offered
*afterwards*, after the decision is made, so it can never read as a price for the confirmation.

**The offer is on every exit, including "I would rather not."** Somebody who declined to vouch
for one person is still welcome here; writing them off loses them twice.

**The privacy promise is unchanged, word for word.** The account they open is their own and is
never linked to the confirmation. Their address is not attached to what they just did, and
RUL-136's promise - that the confirmer is never named, stored or published - stays true.

Ledger: RG-0375 (locked), aimed at the class - any page a non-user lands on because one of our
users sent them there.
