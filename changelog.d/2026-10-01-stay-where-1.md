- **STAY-WHERE-1 (RG-0647):** in Quick, someone listing a B&B, guest house, self-catering cottage, farm stay or camp site is now asked the right things. A new first choice in Adventures, "Place to stay", asks what kind of place, the price per room per night, then her city and its own areas (for example George: Wilderness, Heatherlands ...), exactly like the other categories. Before, she was asked "How long?" and "Best season?" and offered Kruger, Cape Town or the Garden Route as where it is. Typing "B&B" works the same way. Her advert reads "B&B in Wilderness", is priced per room per night, and lands under Stays in TrustSquare. Trips are unchanged. In all five languages.
