📱 Put it on your phone — no app store needed
Android: open the link in Chrome — it will usually offer to add TrustSquare to your home screen automatically. If not: menu (⋮) → Add to Home screen.
iPhone: open the link in Safari, tap Share ⬆ → Add to Home Screen.
It then behaves like a normal app icon.
