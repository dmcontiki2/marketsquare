3  What "equivalent swap-out" has to mean

All five, or it is not equivalent and it needs R4 approval.

1

REGISTERED

The candidate model has an entry on ai_price_card.json. No price, no swap.

2

CAPABLE

gate_by_tier passes for the exact tier -- the gate tuple is (model, tier, prompt version, effort, sampling), never a model-wide boolean.

3

AFFORDABLE

Worst-case cost per CALL within the envelope is <= baseline worst case x tolerance. Note: per-Mtok price is NOT the test -- token shapes differ.

4

GOLDEN

Passes the golden set on the LIVE seam, not on the dead failover/ package.

5

RECORDED

The change is written with actor, prior value, new value, reason and timestamp. Today POST /admin/flags writes NONE of these.

Why the envelope, not just the model

The same model at 10 uncapped client images costs several times the same model at 10 images capped to 1344px. A register that pinned only the model id would have reported “no change” throughout. That is the specific failure this baseline is built to prevent.

The batch-card lister has no resize

bea_main.py:15983 takes up to 10 client images and forwards them as received. It is the largest uncontrolled input-token surface in the app — the caller sets the cost.
