The Harness, Explained

A harness (DeepSeek Harness, Claude Code, Codex…) is the machine between the brain and your computer. The AI model is just a cartridge that plugs into one socket — swap it any time. The harness holds the hands: it is what actually reads your files, runs your apps and touches your world. And the rules dial belongs to the owner.

1 · The brain is a plugin

Swap Claude for GPT for a local model without rebuilding anything. Models now compete purely on merit — best one for the job wins, no lock-in.

2 · The harness holds the hands

Files, shell, apps, web — the tools stay identical whichever brain is plugged in. The harness, not the model, is what acts on your computer.

3 · The owner holds the rules

Permissions are a dial you set, not a policy you inherit. In your own home, on your own harness, with a local model — the whole stack answers to you.
