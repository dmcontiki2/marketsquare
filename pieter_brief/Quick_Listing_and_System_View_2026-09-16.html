BETA IN TESTING · live page, go-live date not set trustsquare.co/quick/
What it is: the same TrustSquare, entered through a five-tap door. No typing, no form, no sign-in screen. Made for people whose labour is the product — a housekeeper, a driver, a tutor, a gardener — and for anyone who wants to list or find something in under a minute on a cheap phone.
Not a second system. One server, one rulebook, one database. Quick Listing has no scorer and no store of its own; it hands the advert card into the same listings the main app reads, and the same three scores apply. What feels like a separate app is its own violet home-screen tile and its own manifest.

Free where the worker brings the linkA housekeeper sends her personal link to the family she already works for. That connection costs nobody a Tuppence — both become app users. Only platform-made introductions cost.
Vouching, not vettingAn employer can confirm someone they have worked with. That becomes a dated fact on the card and lifts the Trust Score. TrustSquare never declares a person "safe".
