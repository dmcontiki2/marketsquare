LIVE ROLLING OUT · v1.3.1 · health OK, probed 16 Sep 2026 trustsquare.co
One idea: TrustSquare never sells the thing. It sells the introduction. A buyer finds an anonymous listing, pays 1 Tuppence (fixed at US$2) to be introduced, the seller accepts, and only then do the two people see each other's names and numbers. Nothing else ever passes through the till.
Every listing shows a Trust Score, a general area and the advert — no name, no face, no phone — until both sides accept. The deal itself happens between the two people, off the platform.

The three scores behind the shelf order

TS · Trust ScoreSeller trust, 0–100. The only one shown.
LS · Listing ScoreAdvert quality, 0–100. Drives order, never printed.
RS · Ranking Score0.5 × LS + 0.5 × TS. Sorts each local band.
Order inside every band: RS, then TS, then LS — ties settle on trust first, advert quality second. Same rule in all eight categories.

Rules that never bend

Anonymous until both accept
1T per intro, every tier
Never merchant of record
Buzz only between connected pairs
No per-message channels (SMS/WhatsApp)
Facts about credentials, never verdicts on people
