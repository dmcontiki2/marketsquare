FindingsBy dimension

Ease — getting around
  • Neither app writes browser history, so the phone's Back gesture exits Quick mid-advert and Forward reloads the door with her answers lost.
  • The "saved" card sits on top of Quick's own ← and ↻ buttons and has no close, so a second advert needs a reload.
  • Escape and the Back gesture do not dismiss the saved card either.
  • Offer a service → publish-ready is 5 screens and about 8 taps, and every answer travels into the draft.
Accuracy — what crosses the seam
  • "Find a pro" invents five cleaners with prices and trust stars, and says "Yes — 5 near you" on the public door.
  • A stranger's advert is filed under city "Pretoria" whatever area she taps or types, because the city comes from the phone's time zone.
  • The Find screen says "Menlyn first" after she picked Pretoria East, and "available this month" after she asked for today.
  • Role, class, area, rate and days reach the app intact, and Quick and the app show one score.
Speed — cheap phone, SA network
  • The Quick door paints in 1.7 s on 4G and 2.7 s on weak 3G, for 427 KB.
  • Arriving in TrustSquare from Quick takes 11.8 s to usable on weak 3G, because the whole home page (1.9 MB of script and photos) loads before her draft.
  • Within four seconds that landing has pulled 3.7 MB, much of it home and "Wonders" photos she never sees.
The two icons
  • Quick's tile claims the whole site (scope /), so TrustSquare pages open inside the violet Quick window and the app's install offer is switched off there.
  • The served TrustSquare manifest lost its id, start_url and scope (the assets/ copy still has them, RUL-123), so a tile opens whatever page it was installed from.
  • Quick never offers its own tile and never starts the service worker, so Chrome's one-tap install cannot fire for a Quick-only visitor.
  • The two tiles are the same mark in violet and green with their own names, as RUL-124(d) set.
  • Which tile each person is offered has been your open call since 14 Sep.

Pass it onHanding Quick from one cleaner to another

Can two phones held head to head do it? Yes, partly today: Quick hands the invite to the phone's share sheet, where AirDrop (iPhones held together, iOS 17+) and Tap to Share (Pixel 6+) carry it between touching phones, to be proven on two real handsets; a small Android shell (NFC tag emulation) later makes it work for any phone, iPhone XS and newer included, with nothing installed on the receiving side.

What actually travels is a link. A web app cannot be copied phone to phone; the receiving phone opens the link and installs Quick from it, so she needs data for that one step.

Why it matters now: the only TrustSquare message a cleaner holds today is her private key link, so forwarding it to "share Quick" hands a friend her account.
