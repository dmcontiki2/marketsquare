## Schedule H — Botswana

This Schedule applies if you are habitually resident in Botswana and prevails over the body of this EULA to the extent of any conflict.

- **H1 Mandatory rights** — Nothing in this EULA excludes or limits any right you have under the Consumer Protection Act, 2018 or any other law of Botswana that cannot be excluded by agreement.
- **H2 Electronic contracting** — Your electronic acceptance under Section 2.4 is a valid electronic signature under the Electronic Communications and Transactions Act, 2014.
- **H3 Data protection** — Your personal data is processed in accordance with the Data Protection Act, 2024 (Act No. 18 of 2024). You may lodge a complaint with the Information and Data Protection Commission. Transfers of your data outside Botswana (Section 9) are made only on the grounds that Act permits.
- **H4 Unfair terms** — Any term that is unfair, unreasonable or unjust within the meaning of the Consumer Protection Act does not bind you.

## Schedule I — Germany

This Schedule applies if you are habitually resident in Germany and prevails over the body of this EULA to the extent of any conflict.

- **I1 Mandatory rights** — Nothing in this EULA deprives you of the protection of the mandatory consumer law of Germany (Article 6(2) Rome I Regulation), including sections 305–310 and 312–312k of the German Civil Code (BGB).
- **I2 Right of withdrawal** — You have a 14-day right to withdraw from a contract for Tuppence or a subscription (sections 312g and 355 BGB). The model withdrawal information and form are provided before purchase. If you expressly ask for an Introduction to begin before the period ends and acknowledge that you lose the right once the service is fully performed, the right lapses on full performance (section 356(4) BGB); if you withdraw before that, held Tuppence is released.
- **I3 Liability** — The Platform is liable without limitation for intent and gross negligence, for injury to life, body or health, and under the Product Liability Act. For slight negligence it is liable only for breach of essential contractual obligations and only for the typical, foreseeable damage. Section 10.6 applies subject to this Schedule.
- **I4 Data protection** — Processing is subject to the GDPR and the Federal Data Protection Act (BDSG). You may complain to any German data protection supervisory authority, in particular the one for your federal state.
- **I5 Consumer dispute resolution** — The Operator is neither obliged nor willing to take part in dispute resolution proceedings before a consumer arbitration board (section 36 VSBG).
- **I6 Provider identification** — The Operator's provider identification (section 5 Digital Services Act of Germany, DDG) is set out at the head of this EULA (Operator name, registration number and registered address) and on trustsquare.co/terms.
- **I7 Unfair terms** — Any term invalid under sections 307–309 BGB is replaced by the statutory provision.

## Law status used for this draft (checked 24 Sep 2026)

- Botswana: Data Protection Act 18 of 2024 in force (replaced the 2018 Act); regulator is the Information and Data Protection Commission.
- Mozambique: no dedicated data protection law enacted; the Electronic Transactions Law 3/2017 and Constitution art. 71 govern; a Personal Data Protection Bill is pending.
- Namibia: no comprehensive data protection act enacted (Bill tabled late 2025); Constitution art. 13 governs.
- Kenya: Data Protection Act 2019 (ODPC); Consumer Protection Act 2012.
- Germany: GDPR + BDSG; BGB distance-contract withdrawal; DDG (replaced the TMG in 2024) for provider identification; VSBG s36 dispute-resolution statement.
