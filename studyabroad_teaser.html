💡 Opportunities the dossier surfaces

  • 100 full Hungarian scholarships reserved for South Africans — most families have never heard of them.
  • Germany charges no tuition at public universities — the real barrier is the living-cost proof, which part-time work rights soften.
  • The “smart sequence” keeps year-one cost at home rates and still lands an international CV.
  • English-taught EU programmes mean no new language needed for the degree itself.

⚠️ Risks it warns about — plainly

  • Scholarship deadlines are annual and hard. Miss January, wait a year.
  • NSFAS does not fund study at foreign institutions.
  • Visa funds must usually sit seasoned in the account — a last-minute deposit can sink an application.
  • Rand drift: a budget set in August can be 10% short by January.
  • Rules change. Every flagged figure must be re-checked against the official source before you commit — your adviser does exactly that.
This example is deliberately honest: two of Lerato’s five routes score LOW on her budget. A dossier that only says yes is an advert. Ours is preparation.

💡 Opportunities the dossier surfaces

  • Cruise contracts pay in USD/EUR with food and lodging included — the rare gap year you come home from with savings.
  • The au pair year extends to a maximum of 24 months — one application, up to two years of growth.
  • Summer camp + au pair or cruise stack into a full, funded year.
  • Every route builds references and work discipline — the direction decision gets made with money in the bank and the world seen.

⚠️ Risks it warns about — plainly

  • There is no “walk-in work visa” for the USA. Anyone selling one is selling a scam.
  • Legitimate cruise recruiters charge candidates nothing. A big upfront “placement fee” is a red flag.
  • Passports and apostilled police clearances take weeks — start the paperwork before the interview, not after.
  • H-2B is capped annually — timing beats enthusiasm.
  • A stipend is not a salary: the au pair year is rich in growth, modest in savings. The dossier does that maths honestly.

🤝 The real placement lane (examples, not endorsements)

  • Cruise: Shosholoza Ocean Academy — the official MSC Cruises agency for Southern Africa; GRI — Gourmet Recruitment International, placing since 1999, no candidate fees; Crew Life@Sea, Cape Town.
  • USA programmes: State-Department-designated J-1 sponsor agencies, reached through established SA agents.
