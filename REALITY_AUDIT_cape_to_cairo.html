The food test — every plate checked

6 for 6, correctly placed: the "spiced mince under savoury custard with syrup-soaked plaited doughnuts" is bobotie + koeksisters (Cape Malay ✓ at the Cape) · slow-roasted Karoo lamb ✓ on the Karoo · "air-dried spiced beef + coiled farm sausage" is biltong + boerewors ✓ on the Highveld · "grilled meat & maize" is nyama choma + ugali ✓ on TAZARA · "fava beans stewed overnight with cumin" is ful medames ✓ on the Nile · "rice, lentils & fried onion" is koshari ✓ in Egypt. Whoever wrote this menu ate their way up the continent.

The two honest gaps

Pacing. Our six legs sum to ~9,000 km (the header says ~10,500 — small internal inconsistency worth a one-line fix) and read as ~10 continuous days. A real overland run takes 3–6 weeks with connections, visas and waiting. Fine for an advert — it sells the dream — but a buyer who asks "can I book this?" gets lane-3 partner segments, not the through-ticket.

The Sudan leg. The one stretch where no operator can currently take a booking. When the corridor reopens it is the single most romantic rail gap on earth; until then the honest product is rail to Dar, air bridge, rail again from Aswan.
