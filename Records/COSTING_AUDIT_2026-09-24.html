If free users behaved at the caps, no subscription count works

At the designed worst case a free user costs $11.25 a month, more than twice what a sub nets — which is why the caps exist. At measured behaviour one sub carries 1,587 free users. The number to watch is the platform ceiling, not the per-user one.
