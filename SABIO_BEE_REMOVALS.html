✓ What the public sees

  • ✓What the remover does — live removal, relocation, roofs, wall cavities, after hours
  • ✓The general area they work in — suburb or town, never a street address
  • ✓Their Trust Score and the badges they have earned
  • ✓A price guide and photos of the work

✕ What is never published

  • ✕Their name and their business name
  • ✕Their phone number and email address
  • ✕Their home or yard address
These are exchanged at one moment only: when somebody asks for an introduction and the remover accepts it. Then the two of them swap details and deal with each other directly. TrustSquare takes nothing from the job itself.

What is in it for SABIO

Membership becomes something the public can see

🎖️

The certificate earns its keep

Right now a DALRRD certificate sits in a drawer. Here it is a visible badge that puts a member above an uncertified operator in a public search — a straight, honest reason to join SABIO and stay registered.

🔒

Nobody is exposed

SABIO sends no list and we ask for none. No member's name, number or address is published. A remover who wants nothing to do with it simply does nothing.

🛟

Safer removals, fewer cowboys

The public currently phones whoever comes up first. Here the registered, insured operator is the one they see first — which is the outcome SABIO exists to push.

Being straight about what is live and what is not

  • Live today: free listings, the Trust Score and its four bands, ID verification, public liability insurance and years-of-experience as scored signals, the anonymity, and the paid introduction.
  • Not built yet: DALRRD registration and SABIO membership are not on our credential list — they are marked proposed above. Adding them is a small change on our side, and the points shown are our opening suggestion, not a decision. What they should be worth is exactly where SABIO's view would carry more weight than ours.
  • Bee removal is not a category of its own. It would sit under Services, the same place a plumber or an electrician sits.
  • Nothing has been sent to anybody. This is a proposal for SABIO to look at first.
