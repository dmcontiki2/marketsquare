A member holding all of the above sits at 95 — Highly Trusted, before they have taken a single job. Their certificates are worth 46 points on paper; the category group caps at 40, so the surplus is headroom rather than score — which is exactly why holding more of them is never wasted effort, and why a generous rating costs the ranking nothing. The remaining points to 100 come only from a verified identity and a real track record on the platform — completed introductions, none ignored. No certificate buys those, which is why somebody who merely claims the work cannot reach the top.

What the public sees, and what is never published

✓ Shown
  • What the plumber does, and the areas they work in — suburb or town, never a street address
  • Their Trust Score and the badges they have earned
  • A price guide and photos of the work
✕ Never published
  • Their name and their business name
  • Their phone number and email address
  • Their home or yard address

These are exchanged at one moment only: when somebody asks for an introduction and the plumber accepts it. Then the two of them deal with each other directly. TrustSquare takes nothing from the job itself.
