A member holding all of the above sits at 95 — Highly Trusted, before they have taken a single job. Their certificates are worth 44 points on paper; the category group caps at 40, so the surplus is headroom rather than score — which is exactly why holding more of them is never wasted effort, and why a generous rating costs the ranking nothing. The remaining points to 100 come only from a verified identity and a real track record on the platform — completed introductions, none ignored. No certificate buys those, which is why somebody who merely claims the work cannot reach the top.

What the public sees, and what is never published

✓ Shown
  • What the teacher does, and the areas they work in — suburb or town, never a street address
  • Their Trust Score and the badges they have earned
  • A price guide and photos of the work
✕ Never published
  • Their name and their business name
  • Their phone number and email address
  • Their home or yard address

These are exchanged at one moment only: when somebody asks for an introduction and the teacher accepts it. Then the two of them deal with each other directly. TrustSquare takes nothing from the job itself.

What is in it for your teachers' association

It is money in your members' pockets, not oursA teacher sets their own rate and keeps every rand of it. We take no commission and no share of the lesson fee — ever. The free account carries two listings at R0, with no card and no trial. The only fee on the platform is paid by the PARENT, once, to be introduced.
The qualification finally paysAn Honours degree, SACE registration and fifteen years in a classroom currently earn a teacher nothing extra after 3pm. Here they are the badges that put a member at 95 — above every unqualified 'homework helper' charging R180 an hour.
Repeat pupils cost nothing moreThe introduction is a one-off. Once a parent and a teacher are introduced they deal with each other directly, for as many terms as they like. A regular pupil is never charged for twice.
Nothing needs building on our sideEvery credential a teacher holds is ALREADY a scored signal. A member can list tonight and rank correctly in the morning.
Nobody is exposed, and that matters more hereNo teacher's name, school, address or phone number is published. A teacher tutoring privately after hours stays anonymous until they choose to accept an enquiry — which is the difference between this and putting a notice on a Facebook group.

Being straight about what is live and what is not

Everything above is live today. Every credential in the table is already a scored signal on TrustSquare — nothing has to be built, and nothing waits on us. A member could list tonight and rank correctly in the morning.

Worked illustration, not a promise: a teacher taking four pupils for two hours a week at R350 an hour earns about R2,800 a week, R11,200 a month, and keeps all of it. What they pay us is nothing.
