A member holding all of the above sits at 95 — Highly Trusted, before they have taken a single job. Their certificates are worth 42 points on paper; the category group caps at 40, so the surplus is headroom rather than score — which is exactly why holding more of them is never wasted effort, and why a generous rating costs the ranking nothing. The remaining points to 100 come only from a verified identity and a real track record on the platform — completed introductions, none ignored. No certificate buys those, which is why somebody who merely claims the work cannot reach the top.

What the public sees, and what is never published

✓ Shown
  • What the coach does, and the areas they work in — suburb or town, never a street address
  • Their Trust Score and the badges they have earned
  • A price guide and photos of the work
✕ Never published
  • Their name and their business name
  • Their phone number and email address
  • Their home or yard address

These are exchanged at one moment only: when somebody asks for an introduction and the coach accepts it. Then the two of them deal with each other directly. TrustSquare takes nothing from the job itself.

What is in it for CHESSA

The title earns its keepA FIDE Trainer title currently means something only to other chess players. Here it is a badge the parent of a beginner can see — and a straight reason to keep the title current.
Parents can tell a coach from a hobbyistA parent searching “chess coach” today has no way to tell them apart. Ranked by credential, the titled coach is the one they see first.
Nobody is exposedCHESSA sends no list and we ask for none. No coach's name, number or address is published.

Being straight about what is live and what is not

Live today: free listings, the Trust Score and its bands, ID verification, and sace registration, police clearance / dbs check, teaching experience 5+ years, safeguarding / child protection cert as scored signals — plus the anonymity and the paid introduction.

Not built yet: FIDE Trainer / Instructor title, CHESSA membership in good standing are not on our credential list. They are marked PROPOSED above, and the points shown are our opening suggestion, not a decision. What they should be worth is exactly where CHESSA's view would carry more weight than ours.

We already hold a verified public registry of 4,237 FIDE trainers, so the title check is a lookup, not paperwork.

Chess coaching is not a category of its own. It sits under Tutors, the same place a coach sits alongside every other tutors listing.
