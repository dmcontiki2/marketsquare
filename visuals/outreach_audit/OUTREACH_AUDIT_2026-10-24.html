US texts: the law

  • Cold marketing texts to US mobile numbers are not lawful from this account: the TCPA (47 U.S.C. §227 and the FCC's rules) needs the recipient's prior express written consent for marketing texts and bars texts to numbers on the Do-Not-Call registry, and US carriers block business texts not sent from a registered 10DLC or toll-free number — so New York is e-mail only, as RUL-252(b) already rules.
  • E-mail is lawful under CAN-SPAM without consent if the message is truthful, says it is an advertisement, carries the postal address and an unsubscribe honoured within 10 business days — every US letter and draft does (Outreach Law notes §10).
