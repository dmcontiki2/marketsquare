The plan

1. MEASURE — to 11 SepThe 130 warm emails are the experiment. Get the first real click-to-publish number. No extra sending: a bigger send now spends the one pass blind.
2. FIX — 11–18 SepPhase 1 shows where people stop. Fix that one thing and prove it, as a class rather than a one-off.
3. SPEND — 18 Sep to 24 OctOnly if the measured rate says 20 is reachable. Refill the pools, run the wave daily across every armed city.
Reserve — to 31 OctNo new sending. Convert whoever is mid-flow.
The decision gate is at the end of Phase 1, not in the last week. If the measured rate implies more than about 3,800 emails, 20 is not reachable with this list and David hears that immediately, with the number and the one thing that would change it.
