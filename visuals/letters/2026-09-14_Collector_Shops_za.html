Your provenance is your edge

Every seller starts at the same 40-point base. Your shop's credentials stack on top — a paper trail that's routine for a registered dealer and rare for casual sellers:

A higher Trust Score means your pieces carry a visible ★ Highly Trusted · Registered Dealer badge, rank ahead of unverified sellers, and convert more of those pre-paid enquiries into sales. Authentication and provenance are your daily practice — for casual sellers, they're the exception.

How the deal works: A collector finds a piece in your inventory and pays a small Tuppence introduction fee (1T = USD $2) to request contact. You review the enquiry privately and accept only the introductions you want. You then negotiate and sell exactly as you do today — TrustSquare never touches the sale or takes a cent of it.
