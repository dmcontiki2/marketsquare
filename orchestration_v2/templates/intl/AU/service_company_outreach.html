Every seller starts at the same 40-point base. Each of your technicians' credentials stacks on top — a stack your company holds as a matter of course:

A higher Trust Score means your teams carry a higher ★ number on every listing, rank ahead of uncertified operators, and convert more of those pre-paid enquiries into jobs. Trade certs, CoCs and liability cover are your standard practice — for informal operators, they're the exception.

How the deal works: A client finds your service and pays a small Tuppence introduction fee (1T = USD $2) to request contact. You review the enquiry privately and accept only the introductions you want. You then quote and do the job exactly as you do today — TrustSquare never touches the job or takes a cent of it.
