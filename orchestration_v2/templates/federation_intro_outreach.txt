Dear {{first_name}},

I run TrustSquare, a global marketplace that opened on 1 September. Coaches, clubs
and instructors list what they offer, and people in their area find them by sport, by
suburb and by level.

I would like to offer that listing, free, to every club affiliated to {{province_body}} --
and I would rather ask you first than email your clubs out of the blue.

Three things, plainly:

  * It costs a club nothing. A free account carries two listings. No card, no trial, no
    registration fee, and the free listings stay free.

  * A club stays anonymous. People see the sport, the level, the area and the price --
    never the club's name, its address, or a volunteer's phone number. Those are exchanged
    only if the club accepts an enquiry, and only with the one person who asked.

  * A club that also coaches online is visible across the country, not only in its own town.

We earn only when someone asks a club for an introduction: the enquirer pays a small
introduction fee (US$2) to make contact, and the club decides whether to accept. The club
pays nothing, keeps whatever it charges, and we never take a share of coaching or
membership fees.

I am not asking for your club list or your members' details, and I do not want either. If
this is useful, the simplest next step is that I send you one short paragraph you can pass
on to your clubs in your own words -- you stay in control of who hears from us.

If it is not useful, just say so and that is the end of it -- or use the link below and I
will not write again.

Kind regards,
