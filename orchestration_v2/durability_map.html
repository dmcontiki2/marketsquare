Durability & AI-Independence

The strategy view of the self-healing system: what the app's AI should depend on to survive years and moves, and the loop that stops the same complaint from ever being fought twice.

The model is the least important variable. Durability = a portable AI substrate + a closed maintenance loop + knowledge in the repo — so any model picks up where the last left off.
