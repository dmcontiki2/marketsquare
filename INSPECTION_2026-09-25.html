How this was done

Nine reviewers read every line of quick.html, ms.js, marketsquare.html, ms.css, the service worker and the backend routes Quick and the hand-offs use; four verifiers then tried to disprove the findings; the live site was walked at 390×844 and 360×640 with every write and beacon blocked or answered by a fake, so nothing was created on production; production data was read (never written) to size the introduction lock.

Not touched: quick.html is inside the Quick lane's work lock (goods-fit-2026-09-25) until it is released, so its fixes are recorded for that lane rather than made here.
